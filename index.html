01 — Overview

Not just a redesign. A change in direction.

The 0 km experience started as brand-specific landing pages. But users came to Mercado Libre expecting product pages—not campaign sites.

Our UX team identified an opportunity to rethink the format altogether. I helped advocate for a familiar View Item Page (VIP), then owned its design from concept through MVP, continuous evolution, and the reservation journey.

The old experience felt outside the marketplace.

The original pages were developed as individual brand/model landings. They struggled to perform and introduced unfamiliar navigation patterns alongside usability issues.

03 — Shifting the conversation

We made the alternative tangible.

Our first proposal to move to a VIP met resistance: technical cost and commercial commitments made a change difficult.

We proposed a cross-functional hackathon with Product and Engineering. The proposal developed by my team—and drawn by me—won the vote. We then visualized a more ambitious end state, grounded in usability rationale, benchmarking, and existing Mercado Libre patterns.

With a concrete experience to react to, stakeholders approved the pivot.

04 — Launching the MVP

Two weeks to turn a direction into a product.

We aligned on what could ship quickly, prioritized the essential contact journey, and handed designs off progressively so Engineering could build in parallel.

03 / COLLABORATE

Sequence design handoffs while development moved forward.

First live release

A familiar product page, finally live.

The first VIP launched two weeks after the stakeholder discussion. Visits and leads increased substantially compared with the previous experience; verified figures are still pending.
